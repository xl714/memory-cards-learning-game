Settings

Difficulty

How many correct answers in a row it takes to complete each memory level — 3 levels (short, medium, long), so gauges of 3, 6 or 9 steps.

Interleaved cards

These cards are interleaved between two sightings of a face when the queue is too short (small roster): they keep your memory busy to guarantee real spacing — without them, medium- and long-term memory could never be tested. Their answers never touch the gauges; their score shows separately (🧮 or 한 han). On Korean cards, the romanization is revealed along with the answer.
